What Did You Hear for Lunch?




This work is called "What Did You Hear for Lunch?".It was created as a sort of continuation of my previous work in bringing attention to the mundane aspects of life that typically go unnoticed. The name is a play on the common turn of phrase "what did you have for lunch", as the piece places focus on the sounds of the kitchen happening all around just as much as on the visuals.
