Slow Morning.




This video work is called "Slow Morning". It aims to illuminate the minuta of a morning routine by emphasizing the gestures made.

The inspiration for this work came to me whilst I was getting up in the morning and noticed the routine that I was going through was relatively autonomous. The ticking sound overlaid on the footage is meant to represent how the routine has a sort of subconsious pace to it
