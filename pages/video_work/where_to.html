Where To..?




This video work is called "Where To..?". It aims to play with the viewers' expectation of where the point of view of the video will end up with each step being a chance to switch to another time and place.

The idea for this video came about whilst I was heading out to run some errands and realized that my upcoming week would have me running around quite a bit, so I decided to record some of those moments where I was heading to and from various places. The POV of the video switches as the current frame of the video aligns with the next, shifting to a new area and completely changing the energy of the composition without being too jarring. I aimed to make each jump to a new locale feel different, so that there would always be some anticipation created for when the scene shifts.
