_theweb_.mp4




My work is called "_theweb_.mp4". It is a compilation of three different old internet computer guides interspliced together to the track "Interactive WordBank" by Macroblank.

My process began when I came across the album "The Era of Information" by Macroblank. Their work inspired me because the way that they make their own music is by doing sampling much like we have done with last project and this project. Additionally, I found the aesthetic of old internet interesting since we had delved into it during the glitch lecture and the origin of bugs. To start finding source material, I opted to search for "old computer footage" on YouTube, and I came across the "Vintage Computer Instruction VIdeos" playlist as one of the first results. I grabbed three videos from there that caught my eye, and then used the Colab Tool to download them. From there, I scrubbed through each of the videos in Premiere Pro, cutting out sections that I thought would mesh thematically and or visually, and then rearranged the pieces to create the final product. I then added in the music, did some slight audio editing and added a fade out to the ending to clean up the viewing experience.

The meaning that I produced with this work is intended to evoke the feeling of the era whilst also calling attention to the things that each video is displaying and saying about computers. I placed the copyright notice from one of the videos at the beginning of the work as a way to poke fun at this being a mashup, and that though originally it was from a copyrighted work, it's being used here as a way to service a work that is made up of other works. The specific mentions of the origins of computers and the definition of output and input are intended to make the viewer think about how interactive computers really are and what differentiates them from the tech of the past. The clip with the description of the use of hyperlinks hard cutting to another clip was intentionally done to emulate the instantaneous of the internet. Finally, the ending of the video being a fade out after the narrator mentions RAM clearing after the power is out is meant to leave the viewer thinking about computers as the screen fades to nothing. I hope for people to come out of this video thinking about the computer as more than just a fixture of everyday life/use, and shine a light on how impressive it is to have a machine that can do all of these things.
