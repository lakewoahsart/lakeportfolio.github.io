Byte character exploration



I made this series of drawings as an exploration of a character that I created for a digital media project. I wanted to nail down both the overall design of Byte and how she would pose around in space when using her shield. I aimed to get across a specific emotion and energy with each of the different poses ranging from struggle, determination, and surprise. These drawings are also used as assets in the aforementioned project.
