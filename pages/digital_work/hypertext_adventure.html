INSPECT - a hypertext adventure



This was my first attempt at a hypertext adventure/narrative. It utilizes a lot of basic HTML and computer actions like inspect element, navigating hyperlinks, and highlighting to see hidden text. My approach to this project was inspired by things that I grew up with and enjoyed that were particularly ARG-influenced or involved breaking out of bounds of normal use of a medium like Gravity Falls' end of episode ciphers that form a larger image, the game "There Is No Game", Superhot, both it's original, VR port, and MCD DLC with their very meta use of the game within a game, and Alan Becker's AvA serieswith how the web apps/tools featured there get bent from their original purpose and used in a creative way.
