Blockbench renders



In my time working on the Fsang18 Heropack, I was grasiously granted the opportunity to expand and write up a design document for some of the systems pertaining to Iron Man in the addon, which coincided with the opportunity to learn to model the new planned additions with the Blockbench program. This allowed me to create these rendered images showcasing the newly modeled and textured armors coming to the addon, whilst also experimenting with creating a composition within a 3D environment.
